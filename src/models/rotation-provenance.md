## What remains illustrative

The numerical yaw, flexion, leg and elbow angles, millimeter-labelled internal telemetry, gaze vectors, exact joint locations, phase timing and muscle overlays are authored model assumptions. They are not source-measured anatomy, motion-capture data, an individual's required angles or proof of safe loading. Left/right and mirror checks establish software consistency only. This source comparison does not validate exact muscle activity or cervical/thoracic/lumbar motion distribution.

Source records, geometry and UI matching have different evidentiary roles. `assetSha256` is `null`, because no geometry checksum has been generated. Pattern references remain `exactCanonicalMatch: false`; frame mappings and professional-review count remain zero. Those statements do not impose professional approval as a delivery gate.

## Matching boundaries

The mat Saw, Spine Twist and Criss-Cross diagrams are not substitutes for equipment variants, side-lying Open Book, roll-back sequences or a whole abdominal series. The matching registry rejects those aliases. Other tracks' fallback matching is outside this batch and must not be counted as reviewed here.
